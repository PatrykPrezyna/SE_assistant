Systems Engineering Assistant

Systems Engineering helps teams develop successful products by balancing competing stakeholder needs, business objectives and project risks.

This assistant provides a practical set of Systems Engineering artifacts and models that can be tailored to your organization's needs. For each category, which may be viewed as a stage in the Product Development Process, users can select an implementation approach that best matches their desired level of formality and organizational maturity.

Artifacts may be created, refined, and revisited multiple times throughout the development lifecycle, often in parallel. The framework is designed to be flexible and scalable for small and mid-sized organizations.

A key objective is to engage the entire development team in Systems Engineering activities, fostering collaboration across disciplines and reducing organizational silos.
